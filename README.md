# MYSELF

My name is **Somoe Adjetey Jonathan**. I am a 33-year-old Ghanaian from the Ga ethnic group, and I come from Tema New Town in the Greater Accra Region of Ghana. I am fair in complexion and approximately 5.8 feet tall.

I was born in Takoradi and grew up in Kwesimintsim, where I spent most of my childhood. I began my basic education at **St. Anthony of Padua Catholic School Complex**, where I completed both my primary and Junior High School education.

After completing my basic education, I proceeded to **Koforidua Senior High Technical School**, where I studied Auto Mechanics for four years. My time in school helped me gain knowledge, develop practical skills, and build experiences that have contributed to my personal growth.

When it comes to food, my favourite meal is banku with okro stew, prepared with plenty of seafood and wele. I particularly enjoy this traditional Ghanaian delicacy.

My hobbies and interests include helping people, offering a helping hand to anyone I meet who needs assistance, and greeting people warmly. I believe in kindness, respect, and showing concern for others. I find joy in making a positive difference in people's lives, no matter how small the gesture may be.

In conclusion, I consider myself a friendly and helpful person who values education, good relationships, and the importance of living peacefully with others.

**Yours faithfully,**

**Somoe A. J.**
